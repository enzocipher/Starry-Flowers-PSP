# Starry Flowers asset credits

The source resources in `assets/` are from **Starry Flowers 1.7.4**. Original story, art, and music: **NomnomNami**. Ending theme “Pretty in Pink”: **Marlene Bellissimo**. Visit the [original game page](https://nomnomnami.itch.io/starry-flowers).

The original resources retain their authors' copyrights. Translation credits are preserved in `credits.json` and the in-game About pages. Compiled Ren'Py caches (`*.rpyc`, `*.rpymc`) are excluded; they are unnecessary for the native PSP port.

Existing third-party notices are included with the source resources:

- Nunito: `assets/tl/None/SIL Open Font License.txt`.
- Twemoji graphics: `assets/gui/emoji/Twemoji-LICENSE.txt` (Twitter and contributors, CC-BY 4.0).
- DejaVu: `assets/gui/emoji/DejaVu-LICENSE.txt`.
- Other bundled font notices: `assets/tl/ru/LICENSE.txt` and `assets/tl/thai/OFL.txt`.
- Ren'Py and its bundled dependencies: `licenses/RENPY-LICENSE.txt` and `assets/gui/credits/RENPY-LICENSE.txt`.

PSP packages include `DATA/NUNITO.TXT`, `DATA/TWEMOJI.TXT`, `DATA/DEJAVU.TXT`, and `DATA/RENPY.TXT`. Game credits are accessible from About. The homebrew ZIP also includes this document.
